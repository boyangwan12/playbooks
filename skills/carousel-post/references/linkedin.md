# LinkedIn notes for carousels

## Format
- Upload the **PDF** as a "document" post; LinkedIn shows it as a swipeable carousel. 1080x1350 (4:5) pages read best on phones. Keep 5-8 slides.
- The PNGs are for previews, a company-page cover, or re-upload if the PDF gets compressed.
- Add alt text/description when posting if the interface allows.

## Is it worth it? (benchmarks as of the Nov 2026 search; methods differ)
- [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-carousel/): document posts about 7.0% engagement rate, ahead of multi-image 6.45%, video 6.0%, single image 5.3%, text 4.5%.
- [Ordinal](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/carousel-posts) (about 216k posts): multi-image first at 2.44%, PDF carousel 2.11% with the lowest reach.
- Published figures range from about 2% to 24% depending on how engagement is defined. Safe claim: carousels beat single images and text; the gap with video depends on the dataset.
- Plan used for EXERGY: carousel first (the story), video second (the proof in action). The carousel's slide 4 points to the next post.

## Caption template
```
<One-line hook about the problem, not the product.>

<Two or three short lines: who it hurts, what we built as a proof of concept, what it shows.>

<Invitation: "If something in your work keeps taking more time than it should, we'd like to hear about it.">

#EXERGY #BusinessAutomation #OperationalEfficiency #AI
```
Keep hashtags to the four EXERGY used unless the topic needs one more (e.g. #Finance).

## Before posting
- Check every number against the product or data.
- Check that nothing implies a real client result if the example is invented.
- The user posts it; never post on their behalf.

## Inspiration research (Nov 2026)
- **Seamless carousels** = one wide canvas (5400x1350 for five 1080x1350 slides) sliced into slides; a line, road, horizon or gradient crossing the seams sells the illusion; keep faces, logos and text off the seams; start from a large sharp source. Sources: [Krumzi](https://www.krumzi.com/blog/seamless-instagram-carousel), [Sam's Creative Cure](https://intercom.help/sams-creative-cure/en/articles/11326061-how-to-create-a-seamless-carousel), [Bucks County Today](https://bucksco.today/2022/05/create-seamless-carousels-linkedin/). Those tutorials target Instagram; LinkedIn swipes a PDF, so check how the page edges look when swiping.
- **B2B cover slide**: one bold claim, the logo, a contrasting background, little clutter ([Oktopost](https://www.oktopost.com/blog/linkedin-carousel-pdf-best-practices/)). Gong's progression: feature, benefit, vision, call to action ([AAMAX](https://aamax.co/blog/examples-of-linkedin-carousels)).
- **Example roundups** with real brand carousels (HubSpot, Duolingo, Wix Studio): [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/linkedin-carousel-examples/), [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-carousel-examples/), [ViralBrain](https://www.viralbrain.ai/blog/linkedin-carousel-examples). Most roundups do not link a live post; verify before borrowing an idea.
