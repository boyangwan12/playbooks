# STORY.md template

Copy this into `video/vN/STORY.md` and fill it in. Show the narration table to the user and wait for approval before building.

```markdown
# <Product> launch video: story (draft N)

## Goal
One sentence: what the viewer should be able to say after watching.

## Rules
1. One idea per screen; each beat has one sentence the viewer can repeat.
2. Cause, then effect (problem, pile-up, product, gap, fix, result).
3. The story is drawn, the proof is recorded: real app screens only for the two moments that matter.
4. Hold time: no app screen shorter than 8 s; zoom one thing per screen.
5. Voice never runs over a click.
6. Every number is real (from the running app, or arithmetic on those numbers).
7. Captions burned in (LinkedIn autoplays muted).
8. Names: all invented and searched (list them). Disclosure: <decision made by the user>.

## Narration (first person, the owner)
```

## Narration craft notes
- First person, plain words, short sentences; numbers spoken the way people say them ("one hundred and eighty-nine thousand dollars").
- Name the client once; a second name is one more thing to remember.
- End on the owner's feeling of control (what changed for them), then the product name and brand line.
- Avoid claiming more than the screen shows: if later weeks still dip, say "time to plan", not "solved".
- Words that scenes are keyed to must be distinctive and spoken once per beat or addressed with the nth-occurrence argument.
